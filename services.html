Before you ask

QUESTIONS.

How much does it cost?

Every job is priced in writing after a short call, once the brief is clear. When you get in touch, the form asks which budget band you have in mind, so we can tell you straight away what fits.

How long does it take?

Days, not months. A set of stills or a short ad usually lands within a week of signing off the direction, and a full campaign takes about a working week. Websites take longer, typically two to four weeks depending on what the site has to do.

Who will I actually work with?

Rory Wilkie, the founder and creative director of Forvr Studios. He directs, produces and finishes every job himself, so the person you brief is the person making the work. No account managers, no handoffs.

Will AI get my product wrong?

Not if the product is built properly first. Your product is built from your own photos as a fixed reference, so the shape, label, colour and fabric hold from frame to frame. Every frame is then checked against the real thing and corrected by hand where it drifts, before anything is delivered.

Is it cheaper than a traditional shoot?

Usually, because there is no crew, studio, model or location to book, and a new angle does not mean a new shoot day. For a single packshot on a white background a traditional photographer can still be the cheaper option, and we will tell you when that is the case.

Do you only work with fashion brands?

No. Fashion and streetwear is where a lot of the portfolio started, but the same method works for any brand with a product or a place to show: beauty and skincare, food and drink, jewellery, homeware, hospitality and local service businesses. Websites are built for any kind of business.

Can I use the work in paid ads?

Yes. Usage is agreed in writing with each project and, as standard, finished work you have paid for can run across organic social, paid ads, your website and print.

Do I need to say the work was made with AI?

That depends on where it runs and what it shows. We tell you exactly where generative tools were used in every piece, so you can label it however your platforms and the advertising rules require.

Where are you based?

Reading, Berkshire, in England. We meet in person around Reading, Henley-on-Thames and London, and work remotely with brands anywhere in the UK and abroad.

Can I see what you would make before paying anything?

Yes. Send your Instagram through the free trial and you get three finished pieces built from your brand within 72 hours. No card, no commitment, and they are yours to keep whether you book or not.
