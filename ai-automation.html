Before you ask

QUESTIONS.

What can an AI chatbot answer?

The questions customers ask most: what you do, prices, opening hours, location, how booking works. You approve the answers before it goes live.

What is an AI receptionist?

A phone assistant that answers your calls when you cannot, using a natural-sounding AI voice. It handles common questions, takes bookings or messages, and sends you a summary of every call.

Will people know it is AI?

Yes. Callers are told at the start that they are speaking to an AI assistant, and the chat says so too. Anyone can leave a message for a person at any point.

Will it make things up?

It is set up to answer only from what you have approved, and to hand over to you when it is not sure. It does not guess.

Does it work with my booking system?

Where your system allows outside bookings, yes. For clinics, Phorest and Pabau are supported today. Where it does not, the assistant sends you a ready-to-book request instead.

Can it go on my existing website?

Yes. The chat assistant is added with one line of code, so it works on most websites, including ones Forvr did not build.

Do I need a new phone number?

Usually not. Calls you do not pick up are forwarded to the AI receptionist, so customers keep ringing the number they already have.

Who builds it?

Rory Wilkie, the founder of Forvr Studios, builds and sets up every assistant himself.

How much does it cost?

Every job is priced in writing after a short call. When you get in touch, the form asks which budget band you have in mind, so we can tell you straight away what fits.
