Before you ask

QUESTIONS.

What can an AI chatbot answer?

The questions customers ask most: what you do, prices, opening hours, location, how booking works. You approve the answers before it goes live.

Can you build a lead tracker or CRM for my business?

Yes. A lead tracker logs every enquiry from your website, forms and messages in one place, shows who to reply to today, and reminds you when someone goes quiet. It is built around how you already work, not a generic CRM you have to learn.

How long does it take to set up?

Most chat assistants and lead trackers are live within 7 days of the first call.

What if it doesn't work for my business?

You try it on your own customers' questions before you pay. If it doesn't answer them properly, you pay nothing.

What is an AI receptionist?

A phone assistant that answers your calls when you cannot, using a natural-sounding AI voice. It handles common questions, takes messages or booking requests, and sends you a summary of every call. It is in early access in 2026, so ask and we will tell you where it stands.

Will people know it is AI?

Yes. The chat says so, and callers are told at the start that they are speaking to an AI assistant. Anyone can ask for a person at any point.

Will it make things up?

It is set up to answer only from what you have approved, and to hand over to you when it is not sure. It does not guess.

Does it work with my booking system?

It can always send a booking link or a ready-to-book request straight to you. Booking directly into your diary depends on your system allowing outside bookings, so we check yours before we start.

Can it go on my existing website?

Yes. The chat assistant is added with one line of code, so it works on most websites, including ones Forvr did not build.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Calls you do not pick up are forwarded to the AI receptionist, so customers keep ringing the number they already have.

Who builds it?

Rory Wilkie, the founder of Forvr Studios, builds and sets up every system himself.

How much does it cost?

Every job is priced in writing after a short call. When you get in touch, the form asks which budget band you have in mind, so we can tell you straight away what fits.
