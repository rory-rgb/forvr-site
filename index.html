Common questions

FAQ.

What is Forvr.Studios?
Forvr is an AI-native creative studio in Reading, UK. Founder-led, with six production lanes in house: video, image, creative direction, graphic design, website design, and brand identity. It makes full campaigns, editorial shoots, product films, posters, websites, and complete brand worlds, concept to delivery.
How is Forvr different from a normal agency or a freelancer?
One studio trains a model on your specific aesthetic, then hand-finishes the output. No account-manager layers, no handoffs. The founder stays on every step, from concept to delivery.
Is the work AI or human?
Both. Brand-trained AI does the volume, human direction does the concept and the finishing. AI is the tool, taste is the work.
How much does a project cost?
Less than traditional agency production, often a fraction of it. Every project is priced upfront, so the cost is agreed before any work starts.
How fast can Forvr deliver?
Most projects ship in weeks, not months.
Who is Forvr for?
Any brand with a creative need. Forvr works across fashion, streetwear, lifestyle, beauty, consumer and corporate, whether you are launching, refreshing, or scaling. Based in Reading, UK, working with brands worldwide.
