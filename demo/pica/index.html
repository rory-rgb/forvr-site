02 — How a two-colour job is made

One ink.
Then the other.

The duplicator can only lay down one colour at a time. The sheet goes through twice, and the second pass never lands exactly where the first one did. Getting it close is the job. Keep scrolling.

  1. 00Blank stock, loaded and squared in the feeder.
  2. 01Blue pass. Master burned, drum inked, 50 sheets through.
  3. 02Pink pass. Drum swapped, sheets reloaded. It lands 3mm out.
  4. 03Re-squared and run again. This is the one we send you.

03 — Paper & prices

Pick the stock.
The inks change with it.

Riso ink is translucent, so the paper is not a background, it is the lightest colour in the print. Choose one and the whole page becomes that sheet.

Currently: Natural 120gsm. The overprint swatch is the two inks multiplied, not a colour we picked.
