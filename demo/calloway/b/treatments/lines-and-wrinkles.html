Lines and wrinkles

Options and prices are discussed at your free consultation

Lines that have started to bother you, looked at by a doctor before anything else. Some people leave with a plan, some with a better skincare routine, and some are told they don't need anything yet.

What happens
A free 40-minute consultation with Dr Calloway. She looks at your face at rest and as you talk, asks what bothers you, and explains each option in person.
Why no price here
Options for lines are explained face to face after a consultation, so they are not listed online. Your exact price is in the written plan you take home.
Who it suits
Adults whose lines have started to bother them. Not for anyone pregnant, breastfeeding or under 18.
Other ways to soften lines
Peels, microneedling and skin boosters all soften fine lines. They are on the treatments page with prices.
Review
If you go ahead with anything, a free check at two weeks.
Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Forty minutes, a written plan to take home, no deposit and no treatment on the day.

What if I only want advice?

That is fine. A quarter of our consultations end with advice and nothing else.

Will I be treated on the same day?

No. You go home with the plan and decide in your own time.
