The studio

We design a few homes a year, completely.

From the structure out, not the surface in.

The model

Fewer homes, so each one is done properly.

Most studios scale by adding juniors and projects. We do the opposite. A small number of homes each year, so the principals design every one personally. The constraint is the quality.

The principals

Architecture and decoration, under one roof.

[[TODO-ASSET: founder portrait — Thomas Marlow]]

Thomas Marlow

Architect · space, light and structure

Thomas trained as an architect and spent a decade on listed and new-build houses before the studio. He leads plan, proportion and light — the decisions made in drawings, long before anything is chosen. A house is right or wrong at the level of the wall you move, not the fabric you hang on it.

[[TODO-ASSET: founder portrait — Isabel Hale]]

Isabel Hale

Decorator · materials, furniture and finish

Isabel came to interiors through furniture and the decorative arts, and keeps the studio's eye for how a room is actually made. She leads materials and finish, sourcing slowly and often one piece at a time. Her test is quiet: whether a room still feels considered on an ordinary Tuesday, not only on the day it is photographed.

How we work

From the first walk-through to the day you move in.

Provenance & craft

The most sustainable house is the one nobody wants to redo.

We design for the long term first, because longevity is the honest form of sustainability. A plan that still works in twenty years, and materials that improve with age, are not torn out and sent to landfill in five.

Natural, mineral, repairable

Lime plaster, solid and responsibly-sourced timber, natural stone and wool — surfaces that can be repaired and patinate rather than degrade.

Named makers

Joinery, metalwork and upholstery come from a small circle of British and European workshops we know by name, specified for provenance, not only price.

Kept, not replaced

Where a building or a piece can be restored, we restore it. We add only what a house is genuinely missing, and specify honestly about what a material is.

Philosophy

Built to live in, not photographed once.

We design quietly and for the long term. A home should work before it is beautiful, and stay beautiful because it works.
