Questions people ask first

What happens at a skin assessment?

Thirty minutes with a nurse. We read your skin under a lamp, ask what you've tried, take photos so we can measure change, and give you a written plan with prices. It's £30, and it comes off your first treatment.

How much do treatments cost?

Peels from £85, microneedling from £180, light treatment for redness from £150. Every concern above shows its starting price and how long it usually takes. You pay on the day by card, never upfront.

Do you treat darker skin?

Yes. Priya leads on skin of colour, we pick peels and timing for your skin type, and every result is filed by skin type so you can see skin like yours.

How soon will I see a difference?

Most people see a clear change between week four and week twelve, depending on the concern. We photograph your skin at the start so you can see it, not guess.

Is there any downtime?

Usually very little. Peels mean light flaking for a few days, microneedling a day or two of redness. Each concern page lists how it feels, the downtime and the risks.
