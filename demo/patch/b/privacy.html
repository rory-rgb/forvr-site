Privacy and complaints

What we collect
Your name, mobile, email and what you tell us when you book, and photos of your skin, taken only with your consent. Nothing else, and no tracking cookies.
Why
Only to manage your appointment, your care and your treatment record. We never sell or share your details for marketing.
How long we keep it
Treatment records are kept for eight years, as clinical records must be. Booking details for people who never visit are deleted after 12 months.
Your rights
You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. Write to us and we reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
Complaints
Write to Ama Mensah, clinical lead, Kennet Yard, Reading RG1. You get a reply within five working days and a full answer within 20. If you are still not happy, we tell you which independent body can look at it.
