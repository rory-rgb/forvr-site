Registration, safety and complaints

  • Regulator Registered with the Care Quality Commission, provider ID 1-000000000 (illustrative). Search the CQC register.
  • Accreditation Save Face accredited (illustrative). Search the Save Face register.
  • Insurance Every nurse is covered by medical indemnity insurance (illustrative); the insurer is named in your written plan.
  • Patch tests Before a first peel or light treatment, on skin types IV to VI especially, we test a small area and wait 48 hours.
  • If something worries you Call us in the first 48 hours after any treatment and your nurse calls back the same day.
  • Complaints Write to Ama Mensah, our clinical lead. You get a reply within five working days. Privacy and complaints in full.
