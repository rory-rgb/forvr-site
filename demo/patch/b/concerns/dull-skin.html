Questions about dull skin

Do I need to buy new products?

Usually fewer. We often tell people to stop three things and keep one.

Is a facial enough?

For dull skin it is a good start. The routine you keep at home does most of the work.

How soon will I see a difference?

Most people see brighter skin within two weeks.
