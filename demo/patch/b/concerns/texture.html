What to expect

How it feels
A numbing cream first, then a light scratching feeling.
Downtime
Redness like mild sunburn for one to two days.
Risks
Redness, pinpoint bleeding during treatment, and rarely infection or a change in pigment.
Not for you if
You have active spots, a skin infection, a tendency to raised scars, are pregnant, or are under 18.

Questions about texture

Does microneedling hurt?

A numbing cream goes on first. Most people feel a light scratching rather than pain.

Will my scars disappear?

They soften. Most people see a clear improvement, not perfect skin, and we will tell you what is realistic at the assessment.

Can I do it with active spots?

Not on active spots. We calm them first, often with a peel.
