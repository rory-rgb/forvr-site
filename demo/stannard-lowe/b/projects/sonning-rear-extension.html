The brief
A 1930s semi with a dark galley kitchen. They wanted one room for cooking, eating and watching the children in the garden.
Design and planning
Jo drew a 38 m² flat-roof extension in brick to match the house, with glazing across the full width. It went through as permitted development, so no planning wait.
On site
Sixteen weeks planned, fifteen taken. Steve was there every day; the family lived upstairs throughout.
The money
Quoted £142k, paid £142k. Nothing was added, so nothing changed.

Designed by Jo Stannard, priced and programmed by Martin Lowe, built with Steve Pearce on site every day.
