The Workshop

Three steps.
That is all of it.

The whole method fits on the back of a card, and it is the same card every time. Here is the longer version, and the part where we say what it does not do.

The card, in full

01

Name it

Say out loud what the feeling actually is. Most of the work is here, and most children cannot do it without something to say it to.

02

Give it

Hold the BLIP and tell it. They are soft enough to squeeze properly, which matters more than it sounds like it should.

03

Put it somewhere

Pocket, bedside, school bag. It is holding the feeling now. That is the whole mechanism and we are not going to dress it up.

Why naming it is the whole thing

A child who can say "I do not want to go in because the hand dryer might go" is in a completely different position to a child who can only cry in a corridor. Not because saying it fixes anything, but because a named thing is a smaller thing. It has edges. You can plan around it.

Most children cannot get there on their own, and asking them directly usually makes it worse. What tends to work is giving them something else to say it to. That is the entire job of a BLIP and we would rather be straight about how small a job it is.

How to hand one over

Do not present it as a solution. Present it as a job. "This is Nix, Nix is for loud noises" is enough. Then leave it alone. Children will do the rest on their own schedule, and a BLIP that gets ignored for three weeks and then goes to the dentist has worked exactly as intended.

What it is not

It is not therapy, it is not a diagnosis, and it is not a substitute for talking to somebody if a child is genuinely struggling. If you are worried, ring your GP or your school. A soft toy with a good idea behind it is a soft toy with a good idea behind it.

Ages, safety and washing

From three years. CE tested, no small parts, no loose fibres, and the eyes are embroidered rather than fixed. Machine washable at 30 and air dry. The weighted ones have a sealed inner bag so they can go in the machine with everything else.
