Further in

Twelve towns.

Every BLIP lives somewhere, and the place explains the feeling better than we can. Bo lives where the lamps are. Gog lives in a town that only exists between three and four in the morning.

Lampwick

Home of Bo, for the dark

A lane of lamp posts where never quite all of them work at once.

Backrow

Home of Pim, for being looked at

Every house faces away from the square. The square is very nice and nobody looks at it.

Muffle

Home of Nix, for loud noises

Everything is padded. The town bell is made of felt and it is still too loud.

Firstday

Home of Wren, for somewhere new

Taken down and rebuilt every spring, so nobody has ever been there before.

Halfway

Home of Tup, for being away

A short row of houses at the exact midpoint of a very long road.

Threeoclock

Home of Gog, for bad dreams

Only exists between three and four in the morning. Very quiet. Good bakery.

Plaster

Home of Pib, for being brave

Everyone has a small bandage and a very short story about it.

Stillset

Home of Lun, for missing someone

Every table is laid for one more than lives in the house.

Kettle

Home of Fen, for anger

Built on a hot spring. Steam comes out of the ground and nobody has died of it.

Undergrowth

Home of Moss, for shyness

You can only find it if you are not looking for it.

Notchwood

Home of Sprig, for growing up

Every doorframe is marked with heights, all the way to the top and then onto the ceiling.

Longnight

Home of Vesper, for the 2am worries

The sun goes down over about four hours. Residents insist it is worth it.
