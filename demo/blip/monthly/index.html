You tell us roughly what is going on. We choose the one that fits and post it. If it has been a quiet month, we skip you, and that is the part that makes the rest of it worth having.

How it actually works

01

You tell us

Once a month we send one email with one question in it: what is going on this month. Two lines is plenty. "Starting school" is plenty.

02

We choose

A person picks, not an algorithm, and it is never one they already have. If nothing fits, we say so and skip you instead of guessing.

03

It arrives

Within the week, with its card and a line for you to say. Cancel from the same email whenever you like.

The skip is the whole product

Most subscription boxes have to send you something every month or they do not get paid. That is how you end up with a cupboard of things nobody asked for. We would rather send you nine a year that all landed than twelve where three were filler, so a skipped month costs you nothing and is not counted.

What it costs

Nine pounds a month including UK delivery, against sixteen if you buy the same one on its own. First one free. Cancel before the second and you have paid nothing and you keep the first.

What members get

New BLIPs a fortnight before the shop does, the option to swap a month for a set at the set price, and a standing offer: if one does not land, tell us and we will send a different one free.

The awkward questions

What if we get a duplicate?

You will not. We keep a list of what you have. If we ever get it wrong, the replacement is free and you keep the duplicate for a friend.

Can I just pick it myself?

Yes. Reply to the email with a name instead of a situation and we will send that one. Roughly a third of members do this and it is not the wrong way round.

What if we want to stop?

One link in every email. No phone call, no retention offer, no three-step cancellation flow. It stops that day and we do not email you again.
