Before you buy

Which one, though.

The commonest mistake is buying the one you like the look of. Buy the one that matches what is actually happening this month. The looks come second and the child will decide about that themselves.

If nothing is going on

Get Bo, Nix or Moss. The dark, loud noises and being made to say hello turn up in nearly every childhood, so they are the ones most likely to earn their keep before anything happens.

If something specific is coming

Buy the one for it and give it a week early. A BLIP handed over in a waiting room is a toy. A BLIP that has been in the house for a week is already the thing you talk to about the waiting room.

If it is the hard one

Lun is for somebody not coming back. It is the biggest, it is made for two hands, and it is the one we get the most letters about. There is no clever version of this. It is just a good object to hold.
