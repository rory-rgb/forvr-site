The Workshop

Three steps.
Printed on the card.

Every BLIP arrives with a card. Three lines on the back, the same three every time, so a child who has two already knows what to do with the third.

01

Name it

Say out loud what the feeling actually is. Most of the work is here, and most children cannot do it without something to say it to.

02

Give it

Hold the BLIP and tell it. They are soft enough to squeeze properly, which matters more than it sounds like it should.

03

Put it somewhere

Pocket, bedside, school bag. It is holding the feeling now. That is the whole mechanism and we are not going to dress it up.

The Reading Room

It is a placebo.
That is the point.

Putting a feeling onto an object outside yourself is a real technique, used by play therapists for decades. It does not cure anything. It gives a child a handle on something that had no shape and no name. We make a good handle.
