What is actually in the box

Three BLIPs, three cards with the three steps on the back, and one sheet for the adult with a paragraph on each of them and a line you can say out loud. The box is plain on the outside on purpose, because a child who is about to go into hospital does not need a parcel that announces itself.

Why three

Because situations come in clusters. A hospital stay is being brave, and being away, and missing somebody, in that order, usually within a week. Buying them one at a time means the second feeling arrives before the second parcel does.
