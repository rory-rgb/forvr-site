Undergrowth

Where Moss
lives.

You can only find it if you are not looking for it.

Undergrowth cannot be found by anyone looking for it. Deliveries arrive because the drivers have stopped trying. It is a real place with a post code and it is extremely pleasant once you are in it.

Moss is for being made to say hello. Parties where you knew one person and now cannot see them, playdates arranged over your head, an adult crouching down to your level to ask you a question. Shyness is Common because it is common. Moss is not there to make a child outgoing. It is there so the thirty seconds at the door has something in it.

The one line to say

"Moss can say hello first."
