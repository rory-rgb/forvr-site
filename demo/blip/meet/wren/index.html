Firstday is taken apart and rebuilt every spring, so that on any given day nobody there has been there before. This sounds exhausting and the residents agree, but it does mean that not knowing where anything is has stopped being embarrassing.

Wren is for the first ten minutes of anywhere. A new school, a new house, a party where you know nobody, the first day back after a long time off. The feeling passes on its own, usually within an hour. Wren is for the hour.
