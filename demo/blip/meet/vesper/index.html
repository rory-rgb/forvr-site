Where Vesper

The sun goes down over about four hours. Residents insist it is worth it.

In Longnight the sun takes about four hours to go down. Visitors find this inefficient. Residents point out that you get four hours of it and then say nothing else on the subject.

Vesper is for the worries that only turn up in the dark and are about everything at once. Not one nameable thing, all of them, arriving together at two in the morning with no order to them. The 2am worries are the Rarest feeling in the set because most children do not get them often. When they do, the size is the problem. Vesper is deliberately heavy for its size, and it is meant to be put on the chest.

The one line to say

"Put Vesper on your chest and give it the whole lot at once."

Habit

Heavier than it looks. That is on purpose.
