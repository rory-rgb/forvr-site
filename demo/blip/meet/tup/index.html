Halfway

Where Tup
lives.

A short row of houses at the exact midpoint of a very long road.

Halfway is nine houses at the precise midpoint of a road so long that neither end is visible from it. Every resident is on their way somewhere. Several have been on their way for years and the town has stopped asking.

Tup is for the nights you are not in your own bed. Sleepovers, hospital stays, a relative's spare room, the first night of a holiday before it turns fun. The feeling is not homesickness exactly, it is that the room is the wrong shape. Tup makes one thing in it the right shape.

The one line to say

"Tup has stayed here before. Ask it where things are."
