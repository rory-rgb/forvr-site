Lampwick

Where Bo
lives.

A lane of lamp posts where never quite all of them work at once.

Bo lives where the lamps are, which is most of Lampwick, because Lampwick was built by things that did not fancy the walk home. Not one of the lamps has ever been on at the same time as all the others. Bo has decided this is on purpose and nobody has argued.

Bo is for the specific dark of a bedroom with the door open exactly the right amount. Not fear of the dark in general, which is a big and unhelpful thing to be scared of, but the dark of that room, at that hour, with that gap of landing light. You give Bo that one and it stops being the whole of the dark and starts being a job somebody else is doing.

The one line to say

"Bo is looking at the door tonight, so you do not have to."

Habit

Sleeps facing the door. Always has.
