Backrow

Where Pim
lives.

Every house faces away from the square. The square is very nice and nobody looks at it.

Backrow is arranged so that every front door faces away from the square. There is a square, it is well kept, and nobody has ever stood in the middle of it. Pim gives tours of it from a distance.

Pim is for the moment a room turns round. Assembly, the register, reading a paragraph out loud, saying your own name to people who already know it. The feeling is not shyness, it is the physical sensation of being the thing in the middle. Pim has been in the middle and reports that it is survivable.

The one line to say

"Pim will be the one being looked at. You just read the words."
