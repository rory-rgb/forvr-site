Everyone has a small bandage and a very short story about it.

In Plaster everybody wears one small bandage and has a very short story about how they got it. Length of story is considered poor form. The record is four words and it is not going to be beaten.

Pib is for the ten minutes before something that is going to hurt slightly. Injections, bloods, stitches, the dentist, anything with a waiting room and a door people go through one at a time. Being brave is not the absence of the feeling, it is going through the door with it. Pib is what you hand the feeling to at the door.

The one line to say

"Pib is doing the scared bit. You are doing the sitting still bit."
