Notchwood

Where Sprig
lives.

Every doorframe is marked with heights, all the way to the top and then onto the ceiling.

Every doorframe in Notchwood is pencilled with heights, up the frame and then onto the ceiling where the marks get optimistic. Nobody paints over them. It is the one thing the town is strict about.

Sprig is for the loud parts of getting older. A tooth coming out, a bigger bike, moving up a year, suddenly being the older one because somebody else arrived. All of it is good news and all of it is a loss of something, and children usually notice the second part first. Sprig is for the second part.

The one line to say

"Sprig has done this bit. Ask it what happens next."
