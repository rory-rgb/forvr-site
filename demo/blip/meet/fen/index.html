Where Fen

Built on a hot spring. Steam comes out of the ground and nobody has died of it.

Kettle sits on a hot spring. Steam comes out of the pavement at unpredictable intervals and has never hurt anybody. Residents step around it without breaking conversation.

Fen is for anger that is far too big for the body it is in and comes out at whoever is nearest, usually the person least responsible. Fen is deliberately the squashiest of the set. It is meant to be gripped hard. The point is not to stop the feeling, it is to give it somewhere to go that is not a sibling.

The one line to say

"Give it to Fen. Squeeze as hard as you like, Fen is fine."

Habit

Squashes flat and comes back. Every time.
