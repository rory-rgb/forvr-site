Every table is laid for one more than lives in the house.

In Stillset every table is laid for one more than lives in the house. Visitors find this sad for about a day and then find it ordinary, which is roughly the correct arc.

Lun is the tallest of them and the only one made to be held with two hands. It is for somebody being away for a long time, and it is also for somebody not coming back. We are not going to be coy about that. Nothing about Lun fixes it. It gives a child a place to put the person that is not inside their own chest, and somewhere to look when they want to say something to them.

The one line to say

"Tell Lun about them. Lun keeps everything you say."
