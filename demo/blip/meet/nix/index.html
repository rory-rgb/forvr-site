Muffle

Where Nix
lives.

Everything is padded. The town bell is made of felt and it is still too loud.

Muffle is built entirely out of soft things. The walls are padded, the roads are padded, and the town bell is made of felt. It is still, by common agreement, too loud. Nobody has proposed removing it.

Nix is for the noises that arrive without warning and go straight through you. Hand dryers. Fireworks two streets away. The vacuum starting in another room. A balloon at the exact moment before it goes. Nix does not make the noise quieter. Nix makes it something you were expecting.

The one line to say

"Tell Nix about the hand dryer before we go in."

Habit

Hears it about half a second before you do.
