WHAT THIS IS.

Selected work · 2026

Most of my work is fashion and image-led. The part I have spent the last year actually developing is the layer underneath it: how you take a look that worked once and turn it into a process that holds across a full collection, a full cast, or one room photographed across a year.

The best pieces here start as written direction rather than a prompt. Palette, light, settings and wardrobe are fixed before a single frame exists, which gives everything made afterwards something to be judged against. That is also what makes the work handable to someone else, which is the part most generative work never gets to.

STATUE OF A FOOL.

21 frames · 3:4 · 2026
  • Self-initiated
  • Concept collection
  • Direction, garment, campaign

A collection built end to end, from written direction through to a finished campaign. It starts as a written codex rather than a prompt: palette, light, settings and wardrobe all fixed before a single frame exists, which gives everything made afterwards something to be judged against.

It swings between two poles on purpose. Direct on-camera flash, hard shadow and wet pavement at one end. Chiaroscuro, marble and monumental stillness at the other. Both have to be present or the whole thing reads as a clean render instead of a real campaign. The palette is sampled off the finished garments and the streets they were shot in, not picked from swatches.

All twenty one frames at statue-of-a-fool.

FORVR COLLECTION.

Campaign stills · one garment · 2026
  • Own brand
  • Campaign, product, identity
  • Product consistency across a set

The first brand we built, and the hardest product-consistency test in the portfolio. One garment, a black tracksuit with contrast blue stitching, carried across chain link, a corrugated shutter, a wet underpass, a stairwell and a graffiti alley. Different light, different surfaces, different distances, the same piece reading correctly in all of it.

The colour is what holds the set together rather than the styling: the blue in the stitching picks up the shutter, the underpass fluorescents and the enamel on the signet ring. Campaign, product and identity all produced from one trained model.

The films and the full identity at forvr-collection.

SEAFORTH.

24 shots · 16:9 · sound on
  • Own film
  • Generated to match a real plate
  • Directed, cut, sound-designed in-house

A perfume, a tube carriage, and the thirty seconds a smell can take off you. A man catches something he recognises, and what follows is not a flashback so much as a slip: a bedroom in morning light, a pub, a corridor, a door.

The carriage, the seat, the position and the light are the real thing rather than invented, and everything generated downstream was built to match them. The sound does most of the work at the hinge: the platform pre-laps the cut, so you are already somewhere else before the picture agrees.

MOTION.

Relay · Blowup · Touchdown

Relay is the technical one. Three tracksuit looks, three rooms of one London flat, and a single horizontal camera move through the whole thing that never cuts away to reset. Each person gets six beats, then the dividing wall crosses the lens and fills it, and that is the hand-off into the next room. Room brightness runs dark warm, bright, dark cool, so the film has a shape before the garments do any work at all. It is cut to loop.

HOW I WORK.

The part that transfers

Direction before generation

Palette, light, settings and wardrobe are written down and fixed before a frame exists. It gives the output a standard to be judged against instead of a vibe to be argued about.

One setup, held

A single lighting setup and a single grade carried across a whole set, so twelve frames read as one body of work rather than twelve separate generations.

Real plates where they exist

Where something was actually photographed, the generated material is built to match it rather than replace it. Generative work sits under photography and post, not over the top of them.

Written down, so it transfers

Producing weekly to a deadline is what forces a process out of your head and onto paper. Anything held together by memory falls over by the third week.
