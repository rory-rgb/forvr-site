HOW IT WORKS.

One flat, three rooms, one camera move. It travels horizontally through the whole thing and never cuts away to reset. Each person gets six beats: wide, turn, two details, exit frame left. Then the dividing wall crosses the lens and fills it, and that is the hand-off into the next room.

The room brightness runs dark warm, bright, dark cool, so the film has a shape before the garments do any work at all. It is cut to loop, so the last frame hands straight back to the first.

Self-initiated. Nobody commissioned it, no brand is named here, and nothing about it is presented as a campaign that ran.
