Motion lab · not linked from the site · four primitives

One grammar, four moves.

The rule the reference sites share is one system applied everywhere, not many effects. Proposal: the whole site animates with exactly these four primitives, each ours rather than lifted. 01 for statements, 02 for structure, 03 for every image, 04 for every overlay. Scroll each into view, or hit replay.

01 · Resolve — for the big statements

Creative that reads made, not generated.

Keenfolk (built by Obys) lands its statements one glyph at a time by collapsing letter-spacing. Ours resolves instead: every character starts as an unresolved blur and sharpens in a wave — a render resolving, a develop pass. Same premium arrival, but it says what the studio does.

02 · Registration — for lists and numbers
01 Comms agencies Slow, expensive production cycles, built for a media landscape that is gone.
02 Influencer shops No production craft, inflated rates, zero accountability for results.
03 Generic AI shops Prompt-and-pray on stock models. Output that looks like every other deck.

From the unowned-territory list in the motion-sites teardown: print mis-registration. Ours carries the pitch — the cyan layer is the model's first pass sitting off-brand, and it snaps into register on the black as each row lands. Brand-trained, as a motion. For the studio lists, the work index numbers, the tenets.

03 · Scan wipe — for every image, one curve

Obys runs all 38 of its images on exactly one clip-wipe and one curve — the most disciplined easing in the reference library. Ours keeps that discipline and signs it: a hairline of accent travels at the exposure edge as the image wipes in, like a scan head passing. Every image site-wide, same 0.8s, same curve, no exceptions.

04 · Genie — for every overlay
Both. Brand-trained AI does the volume, human direction does the concept and the finishing. The founder is on every frame from the pitch through to delivery, and nothing ships that has not been hand-graded.

Already live on the services tiles and the Ask AI pill. Here it is on a FAQ row: the answer unfurls out of the +, and is sucked back into it. Rolled out, this becomes the one way every overlay on the site opens — FAQ answers, the navigation popup, the contact form's sent-state.
