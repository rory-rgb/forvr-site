Talk about AI SEO

Before you ask

QUESTIONS.

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO, sometimes called AEO or generative engine optimisation, is the work of getting a business found and named by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google’s AI answers, as well as in normal Google search.

Is AI SEO different from normal SEO?

Mostly it is the same foundations: a site Google can read, a clear and consistent name, and other sites mentioning you. The difference is weight. AI assistants lean more on what the wider web says about you, so mentions, reviews and directory listings matter more than they used to.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No, and nobody honest can. AI answers change from one ask to the next. What we can do is set up everything in your control, build the mentions AI looks for, and test the same questions every six weeks so you can see it moving.

Do I need a new website?

Not always. If your site is readable by Google and AI crawlers, we work on the one you have. If it is slow, built in a way crawlers cannot read, or says very little, a new site is usually quicker and cheaper than patching it.

How long does it take to work?

Fixes on your own site go live within days. Being mentioned elsewhere builds over months. The six-weekly test shows you which is which.

Does llms.txt help?

We add one, because it is quick and harmless. No study so far shows it changes what AI assistants say, so we do not sell it as the answer.
