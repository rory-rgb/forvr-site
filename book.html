Selected
work

Campaign stills, brand films, character work, product and still life, made with AI production. The casting, the location, the styling, the light and the grade all get decided before anything is made, exactly as they would on set. The production method is different. The standard is not.

Everything here is self initiated concept work, made to a brief set in the studio. Where a film carries an existing brand's product it is labelled as such, and none of them are clients.

01Worlds on Clothes0:18

Miniature figures working the print on the garment. No model, no location, no shoot day. The whole film is the product.

02Two Up0:23

Sunlit street, two friends, garments carried rather than presented. Shot for feel, not for coverage.

03The Long Way In0:21

A whole western town built for one hoodie, shot vertical. Made for an existing brand, unpaid and unasked. Not client work.

04Protect0:25

An armchair on black sand at moonrise. Built to prove a single frame can carry a whole drop.

05Seaforth0:38

A man on a tube train remembering something. The longest cut here, and the one that leans on performance and grade rather than product.

06Rules the World0:20

A motel room and a jacket. Also made for an existing brand, unpaid and unasked. Not client work and never presented as it.

01Statue of a Fool6 frames

A full campaign for a label that does not exist. The brief was set here: shoot it where the clothes actually get worn, not on a backdrop. Launderette, chicken shop, the estuary, a bench at night. Every location, every fit and the grade were decided before a single frame was made.

02Ellyete4 frames

One room, built once, then shot four ways. Wood panel, orange leather, green floor, the rail in shot. Nothing here is a location that had to be booked. This is the argument in one set. Build the world once and a new angle stops being a new shoot day.

03Strano4 frames

A Y2K range carried on real street, plus the close work. Denim, studded belts, the hardware shot at the distance you would actually inspect it. Product detail and worn shots out of one pass, so the range reads as one shoot.
