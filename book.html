Made for
no client

Every image and every film in here was self initiated. Nobody commissioned it and nobody paid for it. The brief, the casting, the location and the grade were all set here, then held to the standard of a traditional shoot. The production method is different. The standard is not.

What this is. Three campaigns and six films, made to find out whether a one person studio could hold a real standard end to end. They are concept work, not case studies.

What it is not. None of these brands are clients. Two of the films were made for brands that have never heard of us, and are labelled as such where they sit.

01  /  Campaign, 6 frames

Statue of a Fool

A full campaign for a label that does not exist. The brief was set here: shoot it where the clothes actually get worn, not on a backdrop. Launderette, chicken shop, the estuary, a bench at night.

Every location, every fit and the grade were decided before a single frame was made.

02  /  Built world, 4 frames

Ellyete

One room, built once, then shot four ways. Wood panel, orange leather, green floor, the rail in shot. Nothing here is a location that had to be booked.

This is the argument in one set. Build the world once and a new angle stops being a new shoot day.

03  /  Range and detail, 4 frames

Strano

A Y2K range carried on real street, plus the close work. Denim, studded belts, the hardware shot at the distance you would actually inspect it.

Product detail and worn shots out of one pass, so the range reads as one shoot.

04  /  Motion

Six films

Short form, made end to end here. Sound, grade and cut included.

All six play here. Sound is on the films, so turn it up.
