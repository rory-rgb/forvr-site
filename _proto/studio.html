Studio

A studio of one, on purpose.

I am Rory. I run Forvr Studios from Reading and Henley-on-Thames, and I make AI-led campaigns for fashion and streetwear brands.

What this is

Solo studio

One person taking a project from the first written note to the finished campaign, without the work changing hands four times on the way.

Direction, graphics, garment work, imagery and delivery all sit with me. That keeps the idea intact. It also means I take on few projects at once, which is the honest trade.

Who it suits

Small fashion and streetwear labels, people launching a first collection, and brands that already have a product but no visual world around it. If you know what you want to say and need it made properly, that is the job I want.

If you want volume content at speed, I am the wrong studio for you, and I would rather say so now.

Where this is up to

The work is the argument.

The studio is new, and this site does not pretend otherwise. No borrowed logos, no invented numbers, no case studies padded with someone else's results.

What there is instead: finished projects, taken from a written idea to a shipped campaign, every frame made in-house. Judge the work directly rather than take my word for it. If it is good enough, it does not need defending. If it is not, you will see that too, and we both save a phone call.

How I work

Six rules

01

Decide in writing

Spoken direction evaporates. A written one can be argued with, and held to.

02

The generator opens last

Palette, light and wardrobe are settled long before a single frame exists.

03

Judge it small

Most people meet the work on a phone, mid-scroll. If it fails there, it fails.

04

Kill the second direction early

Two ideas running side by side tend to produce one diluted result.

05

Leave the seams visible

Work sanded perfectly smooth stops looking like a person made it.

06

Finish the last ten percent

Projects collapse where attention runs out: the crop, the export, the file name.

These are working rules, not promises. They exist because breaking any one of them has cost me a day I did not need to lose.
