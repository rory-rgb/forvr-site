Studio

A studio of one, on purpose.

I am Rory. I run Forvr Studios from Reading and Henley-on-Thames, and I make AI-led campaigns for fashion and streetwear brands.

What this is

Solo studio

One person taking a project from the first written note to the finished campaign, without the work changing hands four times on the way.

Direction, graphics, garment work, imagery and delivery all sit with me. That keeps the idea intact. It also means I take on few projects at once, which is the honest trade.

Who it suits

Small fashion and streetwear labels, people launching a first collection, and brands that already have a product but no visual world around it. If you know what you want to say and need it made properly, that is the job I want.

If you want volume content at speed, I am the wrong studio for you, and I would rather say so now.

Where this is up to

The work is
the argument.

The studio is new. I am nineteen, on a gap year, and building this deliberately rather than quickly.

There is no client list on this site because there is not one yet. What there is instead: complete self-initiated projects, taken start to finish, that show how I think and what I can execute. You can judge the work directly rather than take my word for it, which seems like the better deal for you.

How I work

Six rules
01 Decide in writing Spoken direction evaporates. A written one can be argued with, and held to.
02 The generator opens last Palette, light and wardrobe are settled long before a single frame exists.
03 Judge it small Most people meet the work on a phone, mid-scroll. If it fails there, it fails.
04 Kill the second direction early Two ideas running side by side tend to produce one diluted result.
05 Leave the seams visible Work sanded perfectly smooth stops looking like a person made it.
06 Finish the last ten percent Projects collapse where attention runs out: the crop, the export, the file name.

These are working rules, not promises. They exist because breaking any one of them has cost me a day I did not need to lose.

If you have a collection, a launch or a campaign coming up, send me the rough version. I would rather see it early.
