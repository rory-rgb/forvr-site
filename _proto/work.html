Work

Work we made
without being asked.

Six projects, all self-initiated. We choose the brands and the briefs ourselves and build the campaign anyway, so the thinking and the execution are both on the table before anyone commissions anything.

Projects

Six.
01 Statue of a Fool A collection built out of a written world, carried from graphic to garment to campaign with one character in every frame. Concept collection 02 Grit A campaign study in texture, shot close on skin, sweat and worn fabric and lit hard enough to show every mark. Studio campaign 03 Small Circle A varsity capsule taken from studio shoot through to lookbook, treated as a real collaboration rather than a mockup. Varsity collab 04 Forvr Collection Our own line, used to find out how far a brand world can be pushed before it stops reading as one thing. Own brand 05 Fashion Films Short films for streetwear silhouettes, cut for the feed and built to hold attention with the sound off. Streetwear reel 06 Posters A running series of print-scale graphics, made to test type, composition and how much can be taken away. Graphic series

Nothing here was made to a client brief, and that is deliberate. Concept work is the fastest way to show taste and range without waiting for permission, so every project below is finished to the standard a real campaign would need.

How to read this

No brief.
No excuses either.

Self-initiated work has one advantage: there is nobody to blame for a weak idea, so the standard has to come from inside the studio.

Each project starts with written direction, then a graphic, then a product, then the campaign around it. You are looking at the same process a commissioned job would get.
