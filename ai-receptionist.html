Before you ask

QUESTIONS.

What is an AI receptionist?

A phone assistant that answers your calls when you cannot, using a natural-sounding AI voice. It handles common questions, takes bookings or messages, and sends you a summary of every call.

Will callers know it is AI?

Yes. Callers are told at the start that they are speaking to an AI assistant, and they can leave a message for a person at any point.

Can it book appointments?

Where your booking system allows outside bookings, yes. Otherwise it takes the caller’s details and the time they want, and you confirm.

What if it cannot answer a question?

It takes a message and passes it to you straight away, flagged if it sounds urgent. It does not guess.

Do I need a new phone number?

Usually not. Calls you do not pick up are forwarded to it, so customers keep ringing the number they already have.

Is it only for clinics?

No. It suits any business that loses calls while working: clinics, salons, trades, studios and local services.

How much does it cost?

Every job is priced in writing after a short call. When you get in touch, the form asks which budget band you have in mind, so we can tell you straight away what fits.
