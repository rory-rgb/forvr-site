Before you ask

QUESTIONS.

How much does a website cost?

Every website is priced in writing after a short call, once we know what the site has to do. When you get in touch, the form asks which budget band you have in mind, so we can tell you straight away what fits.

How long does a website take?

Typically two to four weeks from the first call to launch, depending on how much the site has to do and how quickly content and feedback come back.

Do you use WordPress, Shopify or Squarespace?

Most Forvr sites are hand-coded, which keeps them fast, secure and cheap to host. If you need a shop, or want to edit pages yourself every week, we talk through the right platform before anything is built rather than forcing one.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site ships with the technical side done properly: page titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap, fast load times, and a plain-text summary that AI assistants like ChatGPT can read. Ranking also depends on reviews and links from other sites, and we tell you plainly what to do about those.

Do you build websites for any kind of business?

Yes. The concept builds cover a hot sauce brand, a watchmaker, a fragrance, a sandwich counter, a print house, a toy brand, an animation studio and a clinical staffing agency. Shops, cafes, trades, clinics and studios around Reading and Berkshire are all a fit.

Who will I actually work with?

Rory Wilkie, the founder and creative director of Forvr Studios. He directs, produces and finishes every job himself, so the person you brief is the person making the work. No account managers, no handoffs.

Where are you based?

Reading, Berkshire, in England. We meet in person around Reading, Henley-on-Thames and London, and work remotely with brands anywhere in the UK and abroad.
